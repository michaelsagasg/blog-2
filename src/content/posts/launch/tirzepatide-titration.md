## Tirzepatide's two-receptor effect

Tirzepatide activates both the GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which is part of why its titration schedule is more forgiving than some single-receptor GLP-1 drugs. Even so, moving too fast between steps still brings on nausea and reduced appetite hard enough that most people slow down rather than push through it.

### A safe approach for self-directed use

For anyone sourcing tirzepatide themselves, the approach we'd point to is a 1mg starting dose, held for a week purely to let the body adjust, then increased by 1mg each week to a 4mg maintenance dose, held from week four onward. Enzo Meds' customer service team can help confirm this plan suits you before you start.

Zepbound and Mounjaro's licensed, medically supervised schedule goes considerably higher, starting at 2.5mg and stepping up in 2.5mg increments to a maximum of 15mg, each step held for at least four weeks. That supervised context is meaningfully different from self-directed use, which is why the more conservative 1mg-to-4mg approach above is the one we'd point a self-sourcing reader to.

## What each step changes

Appetite suppression and slower gastric emptying both get stronger with each increase. That's also why constipation, not just nausea, becomes more commonly reported at higher steps; food spends longer in the gut at every stage of digestion.

### Comparing the pace with retatrutide

The same safe 1mg-to-4mg weekly schedule applies to retatrutide too, though its triple-receptor effect (GLP-1, GIP and glucagon) is something some people report as a stronger initial adjustment period. Neither schedule is objectively "gentler"; the difference shows up person to person.

## Reading a UK tirzepatide listing

A dosing chart attached to a listing should show the same broad shape described here: a 1mg start, weekly 1mg increments, holding at a 4mg maintenance dose. If a chart skips straight from a starting dose to a high maintenance dose in two steps, treat it as unverified rather than assuming it's a faster protocol, and check with Enzo Meds' customer service team if anything looks off.
