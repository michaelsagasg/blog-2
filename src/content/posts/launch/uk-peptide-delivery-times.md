## Same-day dispatch is common, but has a cutoff

Most established UK peptide suppliers offer same-day dispatch for orders placed before a set cutoff time, often early-to-mid afternoon. Orders placed after that point typically go out the next working day. This cutoff exists because same-day dispatch usually means the order needs to make that day's courier collection.

### Why the cutoff time matters

Missing the cutoff by even a few minutes can mean a full extra day added to delivery, since most couriers only collect once or twice daily from a given address. Checking a supplier's stated cutoff time before assuming next-day delivery is worth the minute it takes.

## Cold-chain shipping adds handling time, not just packaging

Because peptides degrade faster outside a cool temperature range, a responsible supplier packs orders with ice packs and insulated liners rather than a standard envelope. This adds a small amount of handling time compared with shipping a non-temperature-sensitive product, but it protects the product's integrity in transit.

### What a normal UK delivery window looks like

Next-day delivery is standard for orders dispatched on time, with most of the UK covered by major courier networks. Remote areas of Scotland, Northern Ireland, and some island postcodes commonly take an extra day or two, which is worth factoring in rather than assuming next-day applies everywhere.

## What to do if a delivery runs late

A short delay doesn't necessarily mean a cold-chain failure, since insulated packaging is designed to hold temperature for longer than the expected transit time. A delay of multiple days beyond the stated window is worth raising directly with the supplier, particularly for temperature-sensitive contents.
