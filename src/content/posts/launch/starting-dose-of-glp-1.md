## A low starting dose is the norm, not the exception

Every GLP-1 peptide used for weight management starts well below its effective range. Semaglutide's licensed starting dose is 0.25mg against a maintenance range up to 2.4mg. Tirzepatide and retatrutide's clinical dosing ranges go considerably higher too, but for anyone sourcing either of the latter two themselves, the safe, general starting point we'd point to is 1mg, increasing by 1mg each week to a 4mg maintenance dose, a more conservative approach than the supervised clinical ranges. Enzo Meds' customer service team can help confirm this plan suits you. In every case, the starting dose exists to introduce the drug's effect gradually, not because it's expected to do much on its own.

### Why the starting dose barely "works"

At the starting dose, appetite and digestion changes are usually mild. That's intentional. The goal is to let the gut adjust to the drug's presence before the dose increases to a level that produces a stronger effect.

## What determines how low the start is

Receptor activity matters here. A drug that activates more receptors, or activates them more strongly, tends to start lower relative to its own maintenance range, since the side-effect risk from starting too high is greater. This is part of why retatrutide's triple-receptor action gets more attention around titration pace than single-receptor drugs.

### How the starting dose is chosen for a specific person

Body weight, prior GLP-1 experience, and how sensitive someone is to nausea can all be relevant to a starting choice, which is exactly why this is a conversation worth having with Enzo Meds' customer service team rather than deciding alone. The safe, general 1mg starting point above is a reasonable default absent that conversation.

## The takeaway

A low starting dose isn't a sign the drug is weak, and a fast climb off that starting dose isn't a shortcut to better results. It's a deliberate design choice that shows up across every GLP-1 peptide on the market.
